WHAT THIS MEANS

While chat is unlocked, anything running on this computer could post chat in your name. Your identity, your keys, and anything that spends or changes ownership still ask for your passphrase every single time, unlocked or not. Your key is held only in this dashboard’s memory and is wiped the moment the timer ends or you lock. We never save your passphrase in the browser: a saved passphrase would sit in your browser’s vault, sync to your other devices, autofill on every visit, and unlock everything, not just chat, for good.
